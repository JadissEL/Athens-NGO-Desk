# Audiences — Greece NGO Desk

The desk is **not** only for people researching NGO jobs. It aims to mirror parts of the **Greek civil-society ecosystem** for several groups.

## Primary audiences

| Audience | What they need | Current product hooks | Future direction (examples) |
|----------|----------------|------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Jobseekers & career explorers** | Openings, spontaneous CV paths, sector context | Openings, Apply, Registry, Life, Scores, Media | Saved searches, alerts, clearer role × location matrix |
| **Jobseekers in hospitality & tourism** | Seasonal and entry-level roles; Greek/English-friendly apply; trust in employer verification | **Openings** source filter *Hospitality & tourism*; **Apply on this desk** (in-portal); [TOURISM_EMPLOYMENT.md](./TOURISM_EMPLOYMENT.md) | Optional seeker accounts, training partner badges |
| **Hospitality employers (hotels, tour ops)** | Reach motivated local talent; inbox for applications | **Employer** workbench (`#employer`), Clerk sign-in, steward verification | ATS export, fair-hire badges, island/season filters |
| **NGOs & civic conveners** | Publish/promote events; coordinate with peer NGOs | **Events** calendar (`#events`); steward publish (v1); org-member **collaboration threads** | Self-serve event publish after org verification |
| **Public & press** | Find forums, fundraisers, advocacy events | Events workbench + links to hosts in Registry | Calendar alerts, ICS export |
| **Funders & grant-makers** | Due diligence, sector coverage, who does what | Registry (sector, scope, focus), Media (signals), Methodology / LEGAL | Dedicated **funder** view: outcomes, budgets where public, conflict-of-interest notes, application windows |
| **Donors (individual & corporate)** | Trustworthy pointers, impact framing, how to give safely | Registry, Legal notice, Media | **Donor** pages: how to verify an NGO, donation channels, tax/ΓΓΠΣ context (with legal review) |
| **People seeking help (beneficiaries / referrals)** | Which organisation matches their situation, without pretending to be a hotline | Registry filters, focus text, external links to official sites | **Service finder**: guided questions (topic + region) → short shortlist + disclaimer; always link to official contacts and emergency numbers where relevant |

## Principles

- **No impersonation of services:** The site is orientation and signposting. Crisis, legal deadline, or health emergencies belong to **official hotlines, hospitals, Asylum Service, and licensed professionals** — copy must repeat that.
- **Honest limits:** Registry coverage is partial; `meta.note` and methodology docs apply to all audiences.
- **Steward quality:** Funders and donors care about accuracy; steward workflow and audit logs exist to support that.
